EUPHORIA by EPH SAMPLES - quick start
=====================================

Put Euphoria on your lead (or pads, chords, plucks, vocals) and turn the knob.
That's it. Ephy the Wizard shows how much magic you are casting.

THE KNOB
   0-30 %    CHARM     stereo width, a light chorus and warm tape saturation
   30-70 %   CONJURE   a big hall reverb that ducks in time with your track (no sidechain routing)
   70-100 %  ASCEND    OTT-style compression, sparkle on top and a unison doubler

   Drag up/down or left/right. Double-click = back to 0 %. Hold Shift while dragging for fine control.
   The knob can be automated: open it slowly towards your drop.

DARK / BRIGHT
   BRIGHT  fresh and open (default)
   DARK    warmer and softer: darker reverb, less sparkle, warmer saturation

SPELLBOOK (second tab)
   Want it your way? Width, Reverb, Drive and OTT each go from 0 to 200 % of what the knob does.
   100 % = Ephy's default spell. Double-click a slider to reset it, or press RESET SPELLBOOK.

ALWAYS ON
   - Everything below 150 Hz stays mono, so your low end stays tight however wide you go.
   - Auto gain keeps the level about the same, so BYPASS is a fair comparison: better, not just louder.

LICENSE
   The first 3 days are a free trial. Then enter the serial code from your order e-mail
   (click the status text at the top of the plugin).
