Ephy the Wizard (USP)

EPH SAMPLES mascot, reusable across plugins. Ranks follow the knob: Apprentice → Sorcerer → Archmage → Grand Archmage at 100%. The staff orb, rays, sparks, aura, glowing eyes and the magic circle at his feet all grow with the amount. Idle float speeds up as power rises. Later: audio-reactive orb pulse on transients.

The knob

37 rune dots light up along the sweep. Two counter-rotating rune rings spin faster as you turn. The inner star rotates and fills. Double-click resets to 0%, Shift-drag for fine control, 20 ms smoothing against zipper noise.
